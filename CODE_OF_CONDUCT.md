# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and curious. Critique code, sound and decisions rather
than people. Make room for different experience levels, accessibility needs,
languages and musical tastes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention and
sharing private information without permission are not acceptable. Do not post
recordings or code you have no right to share.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, pause discussions or restrict
participation. Decisions should be proportionate and explainable; private
requests for reconsideration are welcome. Maintainers follow the same rules.

Report concerns privately to **m@pwl.art**, with relevant links and context.
Avoid circulating private material further. This volunteer project does not
promise a response deadline.
